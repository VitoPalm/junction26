# Two-shelf bookcase: assembly instructions

Original fixture manual written for Junction26 tests. Apache-2.0, like the rest of
the repository. It describes a generic flat-pack bookcase, not a real product.

## Page 1: Before you start

Assembly takes about 30 minutes. Two people make step 5 easier.
You need a Phillips screwdriver and a small hammer. No power tools.
Work on a soft, clean surface so the panels don't get scratched.

## Page 2: Parts

| Letter | Part | Quantity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Cam bolt, 34 mm, silver | 4 |
| B | Wooden dowel, 30 mm | 8 |
| C | Cam lock, round, with an arrow | 4 |
| D | Wall bracket, white plastic | 1 |
| E | Back panel, thin hardboard | 1 |
| F | Small nail, 20 mm | 16 |
| G | Side panel | 2 |
| H | Shelf | 2 |

Step 1. Lay out every part and count them against the table. If anything is
missing, stop and contact the retailer. Do not substitute other screws.

Step 2. Screw the four cam bolts (A) into the small holes on the inner face of
both side panels (G). Turn them by hand until the collar touches the panel. Do
not overtighten.

## Page 3: Shelves

Step 3. Push two wooden dowels (B) into the end holes of each shelf (H). Tap
them gently with the hammer if needed.

Step 4. Fit the left side panel (G) onto both shelves so that the cam bolts
enter the large holes in the shelves. The finished edge of each shelf faces
the front.

Step 5. Put the cam locks (C) into the large holes on the underside of the
shelves, arrow pointing toward the cam bolt. Turn each lock clockwise half a
turn with the screwdriver until it pulls tight.

## Page 4: Second side and back

Step 6. Attach the right side panel (G) the same way as steps 4 and 5.

Step 7. Slide the back panel (E) into the grooves at the back of the side
panels, smooth side facing forward. Caution: the back panel keeps the bookcase
square. Do not use the bookcase without it.

## Page 5: Fixing

Step 8. Nail the back panel to the shelves with the small nails (F). [The
spacing figure on this page is smudged and cannot be read.]

Step 9. Fix the bookcase to the wall with the wall bracket (D). Warning: the
bookcase can tip over if it is not fixed to the wall. Wall screws are not
included because the right type depends on your wall. Ask a hardware store
which screws suit your wall.
